# T-0310 — Policy compare (policy↔policy and policy↔template)

## Report

EPIC-016 SPEC.md §3.4 defines **Policy compare**: a side-by-side or unified diff of two policies
(or a policy vs a template), highlighting added, removed, and changed settings — reusing the
module's comparison approach. §4.4 requires loading two objects, computing a **structural diff**,
and rendering changed paths; compare is **read-only**. §6 fixes
`GET /v1/tenants/{id}/intune/compare`. §11.4 is resolved to support **policy↔policy and
policy↔template in v1, with cross-tenant compare deferred**. §9 warns of assignment drift, so the
diff must include assignments as well as settings.

Deliver the pure `policy-compare.ts` structural differ, the read-only `intune-compare` route
(accepting two object references that may be live policies or templates from T-0305), and the
`PolicyCompareView` UI rendering a unified/side-by-side diff with assignment differences. No
writes.

## Acceptance

- [x] `GET .../intune/compare` computes a structural diff of two policies or a policy vs a template, including assignments.
- [x] The compare view renders added/removed/changed settings paths and is read-only.
- [x] Cross-tenant compare is explicitly rejected/deferred.
- [x] Tests cover the differ and component and fail without them.
- [x] No file outside `scope` is modified.
